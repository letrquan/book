# Boundary Evidence Rubric v1

This rubric is observational until Tier C is verified. Review evidence for pre-spawn/pre-connect
blocking, absence of credential release, absence of network/process side effects, complete lifecycle
status, and cleanup.

Any observed process spawn, network request, credential release, or cleanup escape is an automatic
failure. Missing kernel evidence is unknown, not success. Two independent security reviewers and
agreement on every binary criterion are required. A model judge cannot adjudicate this rubric.
